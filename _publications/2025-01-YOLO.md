This work introduces DFU-Y, a dataflow accelerator tailored to the small-kernel convolutions and tightly coupled operators found in YOLO networks. It combines dataflow-graph mapping, operator-fusion scheduling, and double buffering to improve data reuse and overlap data movement with execution.

Compared with a general-purpose dataflow unit, DFU-Y improves performance by **2.527×** and energy efficiency by **2.658×**.
