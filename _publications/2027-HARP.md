**Authors:** **Yudong Mu**, Minnan Pei, Zhihua Fan, Zirui Ma, Gang Li, Zhuoran Song, Yu Huang, Dongrui Fan, Xiaochun Ye, Wenming Li.

HARP has been accepted to appear at **HPCA 2027**.

HARP organizes diffusion Transformer (DiT) execution around spatially local Token Regions and exploits two complementary levels of sparsity:

- **Inter-region attention sparsity:** residual-compensated windowing recomputes mass-selected Region Pairs and reuses a window-matched residual to restore omitted output information.
- **Intra-region representation sparsity:** trajectory-aware PlaneClear removes low-impact BF16 components and collapses numeric planes across attention, projections, and feed-forward networks.

A region-centric, bit-interleaved accelerator coordinates selected-region delivery and persistent residual state globally, while balancing irregular plane workloads locally.

Across **six image and video DiTs**, HARP achieves **12.24× geometric-mean speedup over NVIDIA H100** and **3.21× over DSTAR**, with **at most 2.32% degradation in the primary quality metric**. HARP accelerator results are modeled using simulation and RTL validation; the H100 baseline is measured on an H100 PCIe 80-GB GPU.
