FDHA targets the alternating ResNet–Transformer execution pattern in Stable Diffusion. It combines inter-operator dataflow fusion with dedicated processing elements for convolution and tensor processing elements for matrix multiplication.

The design achieves average speedups of **3.28×** over NVIDIA A100 and **2.62×** over a state-of-the-art diffusion accelerator.
