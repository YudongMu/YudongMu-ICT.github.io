NFMap uses a network-flow algorithm with hardware resource constraints to fuse dataflow-graph nodes before reinforcement-learning-based mapping. The fused node blocks reduce routing pressure from long dependencies and provide more effective mapping units for the learning algorithm.

Compared with E2EMap, NFMap improves mapping quality by **1.43×** and compilation speed by **1.14×** on average.
